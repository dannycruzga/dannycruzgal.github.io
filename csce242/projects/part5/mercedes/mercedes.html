ENGINEERING A BRIGHTER TOMORROW

The Best or Nothing

For more than a century, Mercedes-Benz has represented luxury, innovation, and advanced engineering. From pioneering the earliest automobiles to creating today’s high-performance and luxury vehicles, Mercedes-Benz continues to combine elegant design, modern technology, and performance while shaping the future of transportation.

MERCEDES-BENZ HISTORY

Built by Automotive Pioneers

Karl Benz and Gottlieb Daimler were two important pioneers of the early automobile industry in Germany. In 1886, Benz patented the Benz Patent-Motorwagen, widely recognized as the first practical gasoline-powered automobile, while Daimler developed high-speed engines and early motor vehicles with Wilhelm Maybach. Their companies initially operated separately, but eventually merged in 1926 to form Daimler-Benz, creating the Mercedes-Benz brand. Since then, Mercedes-Benz has become known worldwide for its engineering, innovation, performance, and luxury vehicles.

THE VEHICLE THAT STARTED IT ALL

1886 Benz Patent-Motorwagen

The Benz Patent-Motorwagen was created by Karl Benz and patented in 1886. Powered by a single-cylinder gasoline engine and built with three wheels, it is widely recognized as the first practical automobile powered by an internal-combustion engine. Its creation marked an important beginning for the modern automobile and helped lay the foundation for Mercedes-Benz.

NEWEST MERCEDES-BENZ MODEL

2028 Mercedes-Benz GLA

Expected Price: Around $42,000 - $45,000

The 2028 Mercedes-Benz GLA introduces a completely redesigned generation of Mercedes-Benz’s compact luxury SUV. It features a roomier interior, updated styling, the new MB.OS operating system, and an available MBUX Superscreen. Buyers will eventually have a choice between 48-volt hybrid and fully electric versions, combining everyday practicality with Mercedes-Benz’s newest technology.

FASTEST MERCEDES-BENZ

2023 Mercedes-AMG ONE

Average Price: $3.4 Million

The Mercedes-AMG ONE is a road-legal hypercar developed using technology inspired directly by Mercedes-AMG’s Formula 1 program. Its hybrid powertrain combines a 1.6-liter turbocharged V6 with four electric motors to produce 1,063 horsepower. It can reach 219 mph (352 km/h) and accelerate from 0–124 mph in just 7.0 seconds.

LEAST EXPENSIVE MERCEDES-BENZ

2026 Mercedes-Benz GLA 250

Avergae Price: $41,500

The 2026 Mercedes-Benz GLA 250 is a compact luxury SUV that combines Mercedes-Benz comfort and technology in a smaller, more affordable package. It features a 2.0-liter turbocharged four-cylinder engine with mild-hybrid assistance, producing 221 horsepower and 258 lb-ft of torque. It also includes dual 10.25-inch displays and seating for five.

MOST EXPENSIVE MERCEDES-BENZ

Mercedes-AMG ONE

Avergae Price: $3.4 Million

The Mercedes-AMG ONE is one of the most exclusive and expensive production vehicles ever created by Mercedes-AMG. With only 275 examples produced, every build slot was sold before customer deliveries began. Its extremely limited production, specialized Formula 1-derived technology, carbon-fiber construction, and complex hand-built engineering contributed to its multimillion-dollar price.
