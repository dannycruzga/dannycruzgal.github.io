AMERICAN ICON. A BRIGHTER TOMORROW.

Built Ford Tough.

For over a century, Ford has pushed boundaries, built legends, and kept the world moving. From the early days of mass automobile production to today’s advanced cars, trucks, SUVs, and performance vehicles, Ford has continued to shape the automotive industry. With a focus on innovation, durability, and performance, Ford continues to create vehicles that inspire drivers and carry its legacy into the future.

FORD HISTORY

A Legacy in Motion

Ford Motor Company was founded in 1903 by Henry Ford and a group of investors. The company helped transform the automobile industry by making cars more affordable and accessible. Ford later introduced the moving assembly line, which greatly increased production. Over the decades, Ford has created well-known cars, trucks, and SUVs while continuing to develop new automotive technology.

THE VEHICLE THAT CHANGED EVERYTHING

1903 Ford Model A

The Ford Model A was the first vehicle produced by Ford Motor Company in 1903. Simple and lightweight, it marked the beginning of Ford's journey as an automaker and helped establish the company that would later become one of the world's most recognizable car manufacturers.

First Sold: July 23, 1903

NEWEST FORD MODEL

2027 Ford Explorer

Average Price: Around $48,000

The 2027 Ford Explorer is a modern SUV designed for everyday driving, family trips, and adventure. It combines a spacious interior, updated technology, strong performance, and versatile capability while continuing the Explorer’s long-running place in Ford’s SUV lineup.

FASTEST FORD

2017 Ford GT

Average Price: $450,000

The 2017 Ford GT is a high-performance supercar built with racing-inspired technology and advanced aerodynamics. Powered by a twin-turbocharged V6 producing 647 horsepower, it can reach a top speed of 216 mph, making it one of the fastest and most exclusive vehicles Ford has ever produced.

LEAST EXPENSIVE FORD

2026 Ford Maverick

Avergae Price: $28,000

The 2026 Ford Maverick is a compact pickup designed for affordability, efficiency, and everyday use. It combines the usefulness of a truck bed with modern technology and a smaller, easy-to-drive design, making it a practical entry point into Ford’s truck lineup.

MOST EXPENSIVE FORD

2023 Ford GT Mk IV

Avergae Price: $1.7 Million

The Ford GT Mk IV is an extreme track-only supercar created for maximum performance. Featuring a powerful twin-turbo EcoBoost engine, advanced aerodynamics, and a lightweight racing-focused design, it represents one of the most exclusive and expensive new vehicles ever offered by Ford.
