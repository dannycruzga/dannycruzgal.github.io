AMERICAN MUSCLE. BUILT DIFFERENT

Born to Perform.

For more than a century. Dodge has built vehicles known for bold designs, powerful engines, and unmistakable performance. From classic American muscle to today’s high performance vehicles. Dodge continues to push forward while staying connected to its performance heritage.

DODGE HISTORY

Built by Two Brothers

Dodge was founded by brothers John and Horace Dodge, who began their careers manufacturing bicycle parts and later supplying components to early automobile companies. In 1914, the Dodge Brothers introduced their first automobile and quickly earned a reputation for building durable, reliable, and powerful vehicles. Over the following decades, Dodge expanded into cars, trucks, and performance vehicles, eventually becoming especially known for American muscle cars such as the Charger and Challenger. Today, Dodge continues its performance-focused legacy while introducing modern technology and electrified vehicles.

THE VEHICLE THAT STARTED IT ALL

1914 Dodge Brothers Model 30-35

The 1914 Dodge Brothers Model 30-35 marked the beginning of Dodge as an automobile manufacturer. Built with a powerful four-cylinder engine and a reputation for quality and durability, the Model 30-35 offered features that helped it stand out from many cars of its time. Its successful introduction helped establish the Dodge Brothers name and laid the foundation for more than a century of Dodge vehicles.

First Sold: November 14, 1914

NEWEST DODGE MODEL

2027 Dodge Charger Super Bee

Average Price: Around $50,000 - $75,000

The 2027 Dodge Charger Super Bee Launch Edition brings the legendary Super Bee name into a new generation of Dodge muscle. Powered by a 600-horsepower twin-turbo SIXPACK engine, it combines aggressive styling with modern performance technology and track-focused engineering. As the quickest and most powerful Super Bee ever produced, it represents the latest evolution of Dodge performance.

FASTEST DODGE

2023 Dodge Challenger SRT Demon 170

Average Price: $96,666

The Dodge Challenger SRT Demon 170 is an extreme-performance version of the Challenger, producing up 0 to 60 mph in 1.66 seconds (on a prepped drag strip) and up to 1,025 horsepower and representing the peak of Dodge’s previous-generation muscle-car lineup. Its supercharged V8 and performance-focused engineering made it one of Dodge’s most notable production cars.

LEAST EXPENSIVE DODGE

2026 Dodge Durango GT

Avergae Price: $39,900

The 2026 Dodge Durango GT offers an affordable entry into Dodge’s current lineup while still providing bold styling, three-row seating, and everyday practicality. It combines the space of an SUV with Dodge’s performance-inspired design, making it a practical option for families and daily driving.

MOST EXPENSIVE DODGE

2017 Dodge Viper ACR

Avergae Price: $119,990

The 2017 Dodge Viper ACR was built as an extreme, track-focused version of the Viper. Powered by an 8.4-liter V10 producing 645 horsepower, it combined aggressive aerodynamics, lightweight components, and race-inspired engineering. The ACR also demonstrated its track capability by setting numerous road-course records, making it one of the most performance-focused vehicles Dodge produced.
