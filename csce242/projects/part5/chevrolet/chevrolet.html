AMERICAN SPIRIT. DRIVEN FURTHER

Built it to Do More.

For more than a century, Chevrolet has been a major part of American automotive history, creating vehicles built for everyday life, performance, and adventure. From legendary sports cars like the Corvette and Camaro to dependable trucks and SUVs, Chevrolet has continued to evolve with new technology, bold designs, and powerful performance. Today, the brand continues its tradition of building vehicles for drivers around the world while looking toward the future of transportation.

CHEVROLET HISTORY

A Legacy for Generations

Chevrolet was founded in Detroit, Michigan on November 3, 1911, by Louis Chevrolet and William C. Durant. For over a century, Chevrolet has built vehicles that are reliable, versatile, and made for everyday drivers. From iconic cars to powerful trucks and SUVs, Chevrolet continues to deliver performance, innovation, and value for everyone. With a long history of bold designs and advanced engineering, Chevrolet has created vehicles that have become a major part of American automotive culture. Today, the company continues to move forward by developing modern technology while staying true to the performance and dependability that have defined the brand for generations.

THE VEHICLE THAT STARTED IT ALL

1913 Chevrolet Series C Classic Six

The Chevrolet Series C Classic Six debuted in 1913 as Chevrolet’s first production automobile. Powered by a six-cylinder engine, it was designed to offer strong performance, quality, and style. The Classic Six introduced the Chevrolet name to the growing automobile industry and marked the beginning of more than a century of Chevrolet vehicles.

First Sold: March 13, 1913

NEWEST CHEVROLET MODEL

2027 Chevrolet Silverado 1500

Average Price: Around $40,000

The 2027 Chevrolet Silverado 1500 introduces a new generation of Chevrolet’s full-size pickup. It features a completely redesigned exterior and interior, advanced technology, and an updated engine lineup that includes new 5.7L and 6.6L V8 options. The new Silverado combines Chevrolet’s long history of building trucks with modern technology, capability, and a bold new design.

FASTEST CHEVROLET

2027 Chevrolet Corvette ZR1X

Average Price: $230,000

The Corvette ZR1 represents the highest level of Chevrolet performance. Its twin-turbocharged 5.5-liter V8 produces 1,064 horsepower, allowing the ZR1 to reach a top track speed of 233 mph. It combines extreme power with advanced aerodynamics and technology, making it the fastest production Corvette Chevrolet has built.

LEAST EXPENSIVE CHEVROLET

2026 Chevrolet Trax

Avergae Price: $21,700

The 2026 Chevrolet Trax is a compact SUV designed to be affordable, practical, and comfortable for everyday driving. It offers modern technology, useful cargo space, and a fuel-efficient turbocharged engine while remaining Chevrolet’s most affordable vehicle. Its combination of style, versatility, and value makes it a good option for drivers looking for an inexpensive modern SUV.

MOST EXPENSIVE CHEVROLET

2027 Chevrolet Corvette ZR1X

Avergae Price: $230,000

The 2027 Chevrolet Corvette ZR1X is Chevrolet’s most expensive production vehicle because it combines extreme performance with advanced technology. Its 1,250-horsepower hybrid powertrain, twin-turbocharged V8, all-wheel-drive system, and performance-focused engineering place it at the top of Chevrolet’s lineup.
